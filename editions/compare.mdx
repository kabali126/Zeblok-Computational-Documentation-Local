Ai-MicroCloud ships in four editions along two axes — **where it runs** (Zeblok cloud or your own hardware) and **how much of the platform is included** (Lite or Pro).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Lite" icon="cloud" href="/lite/getstarted/introduction">
    Zeblok cloud. Core workspace — workstations, microservices and inference.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pro" icon="cloud-bolt" href="/pro/getstarted/introduction">
    Zeblok cloud. Everything in Lite plus orchestration, agents, datasets and full IAM.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lite-Edge" icon="microchip" href="/lite-edge/getstarted/introduction">
    Your hardware. Core workspace, deployed on-premises or at the edge.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pro-Edge" icon="server" href="/pro-edge/getstarted/introduction">
    Your hardware. The complete Pro feature set, including air-gapped clusters.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
