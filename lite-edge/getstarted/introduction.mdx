---

Zeblok Computational provides a single, pre-configured environment where data scientists, data engineers, and AI engineers can access datasets, GPU-powered AI/ML frameworks, languages, tools, and AI algorithms — all from one place.

At the core of the platform is the **Ai-WorkStation**, built on JupyterLab, which lets you write and run code, mix in equations and visualisations, and collaborate with your team without setting up any infrastructure. For high-performance workloads, the **Ai-HPC-WorkStation** adds Slurm-as-a-Service and parallel processing across four or more GPUs.

You are reading the documentation for **Ai-MicroCloud Lite-Edge**. Lite-Edge runs on your own hardware and covers the core workspace: workstations, microservices and inference.
